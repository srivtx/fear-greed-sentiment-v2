# Fear & Greed Sentiment Engine - Simple Overview

## What This System Does (In Plain English)

The Fear & Greed Sentiment Engine is like having **thousands of market analysts reading everything about stocks 24/7** and telling you whether people are scared or excited about each company.

### The Simple Version

1. **Listen** → Monitor Twitter, Reddit, news, earnings calls
2. **Understand** → Figure out if people are optimistic or pessimistic  
3. **Analyze** → Determine if this creates trading opportunities
4. **Signal** → Tell you when to buy, sell, or wait

### Why This Works

**Markets are driven by emotions**, not just math:
- When everyone is **scared** → Good time to buy (contrarian opportunity)
- When everyone is **greedy** → Good time to sell (bubble warning)
- When **fear spikes suddenly** → Market overreaction, expect bounce
- When **greed builds slowly** → Trend continuation, ride the wave
